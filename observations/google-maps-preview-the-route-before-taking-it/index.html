Google Maps: Preview the route before taking it

Turn-by-turn instructions optimize movement once the trip starts. Before leaving, a traveler may instead need to understand the route and what it could feel like. Google positions Immersive View for routes as a way to preview that journey.

Visible frames extracted from the official demo: a three-dimensional route, highlighted route line and turn indicator; a playback/scrub control; a time control and temperature badge. Sourced: the article describes adjusting departure time to see simulated weather and traffic. Frames extracted by the researcher from the official demo do not independently show the full range of weather changes.

A spatial route preview gives landmarks and turns context. Inference: this may help a traveler form a mental model before departure.

The scrub/play control turns a long route into a sequence the traveler can inspect.

Sourced to the linked Google Maps October update: a time control changes the preview context. Inference: this may help locate planning uncertainty before navigation. The simulation is not a guarantee of future conditions or a photograph of current streets.

Hypothesized risks and unobserved states: Immersion can imply more certainty than the underlying forecasts deserve. An attractive preview can distract from closures, accessibility and the best departure time. Rerouting, offline behavior, freshness timestamps and unavailable-city states are not shown. Launch coverage was selected cities; do not assume Bengaluru coverage from this demo.

What to measure
  1. 01Compare route-orientation and turn-recognition tasks with a standard map, plus planning time and confidence calibration.
  2. 02Test how people interpret forecast uncertainty.
  3. 03Better confidence without better route understanding is a failure.
Job to be done

Assumed scenario; no independent user research. Traveler previewing an unfamiliar route before departure, wanting to recognize turns and plan conditions; workaround: standard map plus Street View. Vendor-stated use case, unmeasured benefit.

Alternative considered

Standard 2D route with landmark thumbnails and forecast labels. Immersion is justified only if spatial understanding improves enough to offset planning time and false confidence.

What would reverse this

Decision would shift toward the alternative if: A lighter preview yields equal route understanding, or simulated scenes cause overconfidence.

Test scope

Exploratory protocol: recruit people who have the stated job, randomize prototype order against the alternative, and measure successful tasks out of eligible attempts in one session. Follow-up recall, attendance, payment reconciliation or delivery outcomes require a separate real-use study. No sample size, target lift or causal result is claimed.

Preview uncertainty before commitment, while keeping predictions visibly different from guarantees.
