Observations

Product observations and design concepts by Thejas dp. Historical screenshots and source limits are identified in each write-up.

  • Meesho Live Deal Window

    Live pricing windows increase urgency by showing discounts that expire in real time.

  • WhatsApp Reply Nudge

    Gentle nudges resurface unanswered chats before they silently fade out.

  • WhatsApp Image Provenance

    Image provenance helps users trace where forwarded media originally came from.

  • YouTube Watch Later Revisit

    Watch Later resurfacing prompts users to revisit content they intended to return to.

  • The crowd removes the risk

    Ordering signals from other users reduce hesitation during food selection.

  • GPay Monthly Wrap

    Monthly spending recaps expose patterns that individual transactions often hide.

  • GPay Payment Reminder

    Smart reminders help users recover pending payments without awkward conversations.

  • Swiggy Squad

    Collaborative ordering distributes decision-making across the entire group.

  • Duolingo: Make the next lesson obvious

    The company says learners asked whether they were using Duolingo in the best way.

  • WhatsApp: Make a chat plan recoverable

    Illustrative problem: a date, venue and responses spread across chat require reconstruction and manual headcounts.

  • Spotify: Make the next tracks inspectable

    A queue combines deliberate choices and system suggestions.

  • Google Maps: Preview the route before taking it

    Turn-by-turn instructions optimize movement once the trip starts.

  • Google Pay: Offer a route for the identifier in hand

    People may know a phone number, UPI ID, bank account or QR code, rather than the recipient name in an address book.

  • PhonePe: Make the funding step explicit

    A low-value payment tool can remove repeated authorization steps at payment time, but the user first needs to understand that money is being moved into a separate balance.

  • Zomato: Split meal choice from order ownership

    One person collecting everyone's preferences becomes the bottleneck for a group meal.

  • Swiggy: Join the shared cart from the room or remotely

    Guests sitting nearby and people coordinating remotely need different invitation routes.

  • Uber: Surface pickup state outside the app

    After requesting a ride, the immediate task changes from choosing transport to meeting the driver.

  • Airbnb: Compare the stay price before choosing

    A nightly headline is not enough to compare stays when fees vary.

  • Instagram: Make being unavailable a shared state

    Ignoring a message can look like ignoring the sender.

  • Notion: Turn structure into an editable first object

    A blank workspace asks people to invent both the content and its structure.
