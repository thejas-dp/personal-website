Meesho / Live Deal Window

Design concept. This is a proposed idea, not a claim that the product offers it.

Meesho Live Deal Window

Live pricing windows increase urgency by showing discounts that expire in real time.

Most flash sales feel fake because the urgency is fake. The sale runs for 48 hours, the "limited stock" badge is on everything, and Meesho's Tier 2 and Tier 3 buyers — some of the most price-sensitive shoppers in Indian e-commerce — have been trained to ignore it. The deal will still be there tomorrow. So they wait, or they leave.

The Live Deal Window fixes this by making the constraint real. Sellers opt into a 2-hour price drop on slow-moving inventory. Buyer sees a real countdown. Window closes, price goes back up. New deals every 2 hours. The price actually changes. The clock is actually running. Nobody manufactured the urgency — the seller set it themselves.

For sellers it is a self-serve clearance tool that does not permanently anchor their price lower. For buyers it is the best deal on the app right now, with a reason to act before it disappears. And if buyers know new deals drop every 2 hours, they open the app more than once a day — which is the retention mechanic hiding inside what looks like a discount feature.

The mechanic is Uber surge, flipped. Uber uses time-bounded pricing to manage demand. This uses time-bounded pricing to clear supply. Same psychology, opposite direction.
