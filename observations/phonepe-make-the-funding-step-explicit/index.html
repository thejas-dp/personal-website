PhonePe: Make the funding step explicit

A low-value payment tool can remove repeated authorization steps at payment time, but the user first needs to understand that money is being moved into a separate balance. PhonePe's launch describes the low-value-payment purpose. Its promotional failure claims are not independently verified here.

Visible official sequence includes bank/funding selection and a success screen saying ₹2,000 was added to UPI Lite. The funding screen includes an unsupported-bank state and a note about which account can be used for future additions. Sourced launch limits, including the then ₹200 transaction limit and ₹2,000 balance/load context, are historical, not current policy advice.

Activation brings account choice and amount together at the balance-funding boundary. This is a consequential money step, not an ordinary onboarding preference.

The disabled unsupported-bank treatment shows eligibility in place rather than pretending all accounts work.

The success state names the action and the balance product, with View Details and Done. Inference: explicit funding confirmation may prevent users mistaking it for a merchant payment.

Hypothesized risks and unobserved states: Faster later payments move authorization attention earlier. Users can confuse bank balance, Lite balance and a completed merchant transaction. PIN behavior during funding, failed top-up recovery, reconciliation, deactivation/refund and a merchant payment are not shown. Do not claim the shown success proves a purchase was paid.

What to measure
  1. 01Check whether users can explain where funds now sit, the amount added and how to retrieve transaction details.
  2. 02Measure payment success only with actual reconciliation.
  3. 03Guard against accidental top-ups, unrecovered balances and duplicate adds after ambiguous failure.
Job to be done

Assumed scenario; no independent user research. Bank-account holder enabling Lite for frequent small payments, wanting to know where money moves; workaround: ordinary UPI authorization per payment. Launch-stated purpose, unmeasured comprehension.

Alternative considered

Stay on ordinary UPI, or show a dedicated balance explainer before funding. Lite is justified only by actual later payment reliability without balance confusion.

What would reverse this

Decision would shift toward the alternative if: Users cannot explain funding versus paying, or faster payments are offset by reconciliation/recovery errors.

Test scope

Exploratory protocol: recruit people who have the stated job, randomize prototype order against the alternative, and measure successful tasks out of eligible attempts in one session. Follow-up recall, attendance, payment reconciliation or delivery outcomes require a separate real-use study. No sample size, target lift or causal result is claimed.

A funding confirmation should name where value moved, without implying a merchant purchase.
