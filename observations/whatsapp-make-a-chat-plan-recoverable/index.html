WhatsApp: Make a chat plan recoverable

Illustrative problem: a date, venue and responses spread across chat require reconstruction and manual headcounts. The launch image shows an organizer asking people to RSVP beside a structured event card.

Visible: a pinned event, a chat card with title, date/time, location, count and Respond. The response menu and creation form are not shown. Sourced: anyone within an eligible Community-linked group can create events, guests can respond, find events in group information and receive a reminder near the event. The May 2024 source scopes the initial release to Community-linked groups, with other groups to follow.

A card puts plan fields together. Inference: this may reduce reconstruction from separate messages.

Respond is event-specific, but its options are not visible. The count is response status, not proof of attendance.

The pinned event and the documented group-information route offer recovery after conversation moves on. Inference: this may reduce repeated searching through chat. Announcement replies are an adjacent feature, not part of the event flow.

Hypothesized risks and unobserved states: Counts can encourage social pressure or be mistaken for final headcounts. Time/venue edits need clear change communication. Edit history, cancellations, timezone treatment, RSVP alternatives, response privacy and delivered reminders are not shown, so these are audit questions rather than proven defects.

What to measure
  1. 01Test plan retrieval and organizer time to resolve headcount.
  2. 02Guard against wrong-time/venue arrivals and unnoticed edits.
  3. 03Separate notification delivery from comprehension; response rate alone can reward accidental taps.
Job to be done

Assumed scenario; no independent user research. Group organizer checking a plan after a busy chat, wanting stable time/venue and responses; workaround: pin a plain message and count replies. Hypothesized cost.

Alternative considered

Pin a text checklist or use a calendar invite. The card wins only when in-chat retrieval and response state beat the overhead of another event surface.

What would reverse this

Decision would shift toward the alternative if: Pinned text provides equally accurate retrieval and lower organizer effort, or edits go unnoticed in the card.

Test scope

Exploratory protocol: recruit people who have the stated job, randomize prototype order against the alternative, and measure successful tasks out of eligible attempts in one session. Follow-up recall, attendance, payment reconciliation or delivery outcomes require a separate real-use study. No sample size, target lift or causal result is claimed.

Give chat commitments durable fields and a recoverable state, without confusing a response with attendance.
