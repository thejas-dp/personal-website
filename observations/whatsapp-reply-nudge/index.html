Design concept. This is a proposed idea, not a claim that the product offers it.

WhatsApp Reply Nudge

Gentle nudges resurface unanswered chats before they silently fade out.

Everyone has a Rahul. Someone who messaged 12 days ago, got read, and never got a reply — not because you don't care, but because you opened it at a bad moment, meant to reply later, and then the chat got buried under everything else. The intention was there. The follow-through wasn't.

WhatsApp already knows this happened. It has the read receipt, the timestamp, and the silence that followed. The Reply Nudge just surfaces that information at the right moment — not as a notification that interrupts you, but as a banner inside the app when you're already there. Soft enough to dismiss, specific enough to actually trigger action.

The ghost emoji is doing quiet design work. It makes a potentially guilt-inducing nudge feel light instead of accusatory. You haven't replied in 12 days — that could read as a passive-aggressive reminder. With the ghost it reads as the app gently teasing you. Same information, completely different emotional register.

Two options: Remind me later, or Open chat. No pressure. Just an easy on-ramp back into a conversation that drifted.

WhatsApp solved sending. It never solved the drifted conversation — the one that got read and then lost to the scroll. Every relationship has a graveyard of those. The Reply Nudge is just a retrieval mechanism for conversations worth having, delivered at the moment when acting on it costs the least.
