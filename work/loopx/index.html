LOOPX
Agentic Growth Strategist for D2C Brands

The Problem

D2C brands above the ₹5 crore monthly revenue threshold face a specific and expensive problem. They are spending heavily on retention — discounts, campaigns, winback flows, reactivation sequences — but they are applying it uniformly. A blanket 20 percent discount goes to the highest-margin loyal customer and to the chronic returner who was never profitable in the first place. One builds the business. The other quietly destroys it.

The deeper problem is that the strategic thinking required to fix this — identifying which customers are worth saving, at what margin, through which channel, with what offer — is entirely manual. A human strategist can analyse 15 to 20 customer variables when building a segment. They can produce 2 to 3 comprehensive campaigns a week if they are exceptional. And they cost ₹15–25 lakh a year to hire and retain, assuming you can find them.

Most mid-market D2C brands cannot afford a dedicated growth strategist. The ones that hire one get a person who is already at capacity managing the existing stack — Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta, Google — and cannot get to the analytical depth that actually drives compounding retention improvements. The result is a retention operation that looks busy but is structurally bleeding margin.

The Solution

LoopX is an agentic growth intelligence platform — a system of six specialised AI agents that together perform the complete role of a human growth strategist, continuously, at the depth a human team cannot match, and at a fraction of the cost. This is not a tool that assists. It is not a copilot that suggests. It is a system that autonomously analyses customer data, identifies growth opportunities, creates margin-aware campaign strategies, designs segment-specific offers, and produces ready-to-execute plans — every day, not quarterly.

The six agents work as a continuous feedback loop, each handling a specific function and passing output to the next.

Insights Agent

Monitors over 100 customer features daily — behavioural, transactional, and engagement signals — and flags opportunities ranked by potential revenue and margin impact. If revenue drops 12 percent on a Tuesday, this agent identifies whether it is a traffic issue, a conversion problem, or a specific high-value segment churning faster than usual. It surfaces the why behind every spike and dip before it becomes an emergency. What it replaces is manual dashboard monitoring, weekly reporting delays, and the gut feeling that something is wrong but nobody can pinpoint what.

Strategy Agent

Takes what the Insights Agent surfaces and transforms it into multi-campaign growth strategies tailored to the brand's specific customers and margins. It generates mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive campaign bundles — ensuring comprehensive coverage without audience overlap and without the strategic gaps that manual planning almost always produces. Every strategy factors in customer segments, seasonal patterns, inventory levels, and margin impact. This is not generic playbook advice. It is a strategy built from the brand's actual data.

Segmentation Agent

Builds precise customer segments with SQL queries, human-readable definitions, and live audience counts. Where a human analyst might work with 3 to 5 segments based on RFM, this agent operates across 100+ features simultaneously — producing segments that reflect real behavioural nuance rather than blunt demographic groupings. Crucially, it also flags which at-risk customers are worth saving and which unprofitable customers should be allowed to churn naturally. This single capability can save brands hundreds of thousands of rupees in wasted retention spend annually.

Offers Agent

Designs margin-aware offers per segment, balancing conversion probability against profitability. This is the agent that ends the blanket discount problem. Different segments get different offers — not because a human analyst had time to think about it, but because the system calculates the optimal incentive for each audience based on their purchase history, margin profile, price sensitivity, and churn risk. An offer that makes sense for a high-LTV lapsed customer is different from what makes sense for a low-margin frequent returner.

Playbook Agent

Converts strategies into ready-to-execute campaign plans with timing, channel orchestration, copy direction, and creative requirements. It eliminates the back-and-forth between strategy and implementation by generating campaign specs that the brand's team or their existing marketing automation — Klaviyo, MoEngage, Meta, Google — can deploy immediately. Execution speed is a genuine competitive advantage at this scale, and the Playbook Agent is designed to compress the time from insight to live campaign.

Performance Agent

Monitors live campaign results in real time and feeds learnings back to the Insights Agent — closing the loop. Every campaign makes the system smarter. Every result updates the strategy for the next cycle. This is the compounding mechanism: the system does not just run campaigns, it continuously improves the strategy behind them based on what actually worked. The platform integrates directly with the existing brand stack — Shopify for transaction data, Klaviyo or MoEngage for campaign execution, Meta and Google for acquisition data — through a BigQuery warehouse. Brands do not replace their tools. LoopX adds intelligence on top of them.

I owned the full product surface across the agent system — the analytical framework for what each agent surfaces and why, the orchestration logic between agents, the brand-facing UX for reviewing and approving agent outputs, the integration architecture, the margin-aware offer engine, PRDs, user stories, acceptance criteria, and go-to-market positioning.

Every campaign makes the system smarter. The longer a brand runs on LoopX, the higher the floor on every future campaign.

Workflow

The system runs continuously without requiring a human to initiate each cycle. Every day, the Insights Agent processes the brand's full customer and transaction data, monitoring 100+ features and flagging the highest-impact opportunities available. If a cohort of high-margin customers in the D30 to D60 window is showing elevated browse activity without purchase, that signal surfaces immediately — ranked by its estimated revenue impact if acted on.

The Strategy Agent picks up those signals and generates a set of campaign strategies. Each strategy is accompanied by the rationale, the target segment, the recommended channel mix, the expected impact range, and the margin implications. The brand's team reviews and approves. For brands that want fully autonomous execution, the system can push directly to their campaign tools without a human approval step.

The Segmentation Agent builds the precise audiences for each approved strategy. The Offers Agent designs the specific incentive for each segment. The Playbook Agent generates the full campaign brief — timing sequence, channel schedule, copy direction, creative requirements — ready for immediate deployment. Once the campaign is live, the Performance Agent monitors it in real time, catching underperformance early and feeding results back into the next cycle. The loop closes. The next cycle is smarter than the last.

Impact

On cost, the platform replaces a capability that would otherwise require a dedicated growth strategist at ₹15–25 lakh annually, a retention analyst, and significant campaign management overhead. The platform operates at a fraction of that cost with greater analytical depth and no capacity ceiling.

On velocity, the agent system runs every day. Human strategists plan in weekly or monthly cycles. The compounding effect of daily analysis and continuous optimisation produces a structural strategic advantage that grows over time. Brands that deploy the full agent system are running 10+ experiments per week versus the 2 to 3 a human team can manage at the same budget level.

On margin, the Offers Agent and Segmentation Agent together address the most expensive structural problem in D2C retention: blanket discounting. By designing margin-aware offers at the segment level, brands stop giving unnecessary discounts to customers who would have purchased anyway and stop spending retention budget on customers who were never profitable. Across pilot brands, wasted retention spend reduced by an estimated 40%.

On scale, the system handles analytical complexity that grows with the brand without requiring additional headcount. A brand at ₹5 crore monthly revenue and a brand at ₹50 crore monthly revenue run the same agent system with the same team overhead. The intelligence scales. The cost does not.

Risks and Mitigation

Brand trust in autonomous AI. Asking a D2C founder to let an AI system design offers and deploy campaigns without human review is a significant trust ask. The mitigation is a graduated autonomy model — the system starts in review mode, where every recommendation requires human approval before deployment. As the brand builds confidence in the output quality, they can progressively enable autonomous execution for specific campaign types.

Margin model accuracy. The entire value proposition rests on margin-aware decision making — and that requires clean, accurate margin data. Brands with messy product cost data will get imprecise margin signals. The mitigation is a structured onboarding process with a margin data audit before the agents go live, and conservative margin assumptions by default until the data is verified.

Brand voice drift. If the Playbook Agent generates campaign briefs that are inconsistent with the brand's tone and creative standards, campaigns become generic and erode the brand relationship. The mitigation is brand voice configuration at onboarding — style guidelines, tone parameters, and creative constraints that bound what the agent can generate — combined with a creative review step for all outward-facing copy.

Point solution perception. The risk of being perceived as a retention tool rather than a strategic growth layer. The mitigation is positioning and product expansion — framing LoopX from day one as the growth intelligence layer across acquisition efficiency and retention profitability, and building the agent capabilities to match that framing over time.

The Insight

The bottleneck in D2C retention is not execution — most brands have Klaviyo, have MoEngage, have Meta and Google running. The bottleneck is strategic thinking: the continuous, margin-aware analysis of which customers to target, with what, at what cost, and when. That work has always required a human with time, skill, and data access. Most mid-market brands do not have all three simultaneously. LoopX is built on the bet that agentic AI can perform that strategic function — not as a copilot, but as the system itself.

The compounding mechanism is the most important architectural decision. An agent system that only runs campaigns is a tool. An agent system where every campaign result feeds back into the next cycle's strategy is a learning machine. The longer a brand runs on LoopX, the better the system's understanding of what works for their specific customers — and the higher the floor on every future campaign.
